# Business Insights

## Summary

This analysis reviews customer orders performance after data quality cleaned before they are used in business decisions.

## Key Findings

1. **Order Value Focus**

   House generated the highest revenue. This category should be reviewed further to understand which products or regions are driving performance.

2. **Customer Segments**

   Male and middle aged had the strongest customers by revenue. This suggests the business should reviewing customer segments that generate high-value before deciding which cutomers deserves more focus.

3. **Regional Performance**

   Nottingham was the strongest region by revenue. This region can be used as a benchmark when reviewing lower-performing regions.

4. **Loyalty Member Performance**

   Loyalty members was spending most rather than the non-loyalty members. This suggest the business can promote exclusive benefits for customers with loyalty member.

5. **Data Quality**

   40.002 rows needed review before dashboarding. This matters because missing values, duplicate orders, and non-completed statuses can distort the final metrics.

## Recommendation

Use clean rows for the main dashboard,the business should review non-clean rows before using them for final reporting, especially if they affect revenue, profit, or order completion.
